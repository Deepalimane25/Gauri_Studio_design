FOUNDER

Gauri Designing Studio is the story of a homemaker who turned her love for sarees into a studio of her own.

GAURI Studio is shaped by a belief that what we wear can hold meaning beyond the occasion. It can reflect our roots, our changing identities, and the moments we want to remember.

The studio brings that belief into the experience of choosing a saree, considering a blouse design, and creating a look that feels personal rather than simply following a trend.

“The best part of getting dressed is feeling like yourself.”
